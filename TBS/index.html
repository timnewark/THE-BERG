Headings are matched whatever the capitalisation. A row is skipped when Unique No., ELR, ID, Mileage, Chains and YR6 Exam are all blank, or when ELR reads “TM list” or “ASAP”. After loading, the page lists any headings it could not find.

The downloaded workbook has the same sheets and data; cell formatting, formulas and macros are not kept, so if your workbank has those, copy the three columns from the table back into your own file instead.

3. Print the TBS documents

Placeholders the template can use

Type these into the Word template exactly, including the plus signs: ++TBS reference++, ++Structure++, ++Structure type++, ++Exam type++, ++Report date++, ++Date of works++, ++Exam date++, ++Examiner++, ++Signature++ (replaced by the image), ++Exam area++, ++OS grid ref++, ++Postcode++, ++what3words++, ++Nearest A&E++, ++Welfare++, ++WPP reference++, ++Asset ID++, ++Mileage++, ++Chains++, ++Structure name++.

Building runs the location lookup first if you haven't already, so postcode, what3words and nearest A&E are in the documents. Use a different template…

Grid refs are converted at the centre of their square (a 6-figure ref is a 100 m square). Postcode is the nearest within 2 km (postcodes.io). A&E is the nearest consultant-led 24-hour emergency department from a built-in UK list (Nov 2026), straight-line distance. Welfare is the nearest petrol station, supermarket or services within 10 km from OpenStreetMap (falling back to a café, fast food or convenience shop), so check it is actually open to the public. Check before relying on any of it for a RAMS.
